How it works

On our floor we share kitchen and trash duty and keep count of who empties the dishwasher. This site splits the duties fairly and keeps track of who did what.

The duties

Kitchen duty: every day, tidy up and clean the shared kitchen. {% if kitchen_people > 1 %}{{ kitchen_people }} people are assigned, but only one of them has to clean. Agree who cleans; whoever cleans clicks “I cleaned it”. If nobody does by {{ deadline_hour }}:00 the next morning, you both get a strike.{% endif %}

Trash duty: for one week (Monday to Sunday), take out the trash. {% if trash_people > 1 %}{{ trash_people }} people are assigned, one of them is enough.{% endif %}

The floor speaker will tell you what exactly is part of it.

When is it my turn?

The site assigns duties automatically: it's the turn of whoever has cleaned the least so far, relative to everyone else. If you're new, you start at zero.

The overview shows today's duties and the plan for the next {{ preview_days }} days. Your own name is highlighted. The plan can still change, e.g. when someone misses a duty or is away.

New duties are given out at {{ deadline_hour }}:05 each morning; until then the overview shows yesterday's.

Until today's kitchen duty is marked done, the plan depends on who cleans. The buttons above the kitchen preview switch between the cases.

Marking a duty as done

Once you've cleaned, click I cleaned it next to your name on the overview. Only whoever actually cleaned marks it.

Deadline: {{ deadline_hour }}:00 on the day after the duty, so for trash duty the Monday after your week. After that the duty counts as missed.

What do the statuses mean?

openNot marked yet, the deadline hasn't passed.
doneCleaned and marked.
not neededSomeone else on your duty cleaned. No strike, but it doesn't count as cleaned either.
missedNobody marked it by the deadline. Everyone assigned gets a strike.
disputedMarked, but not cleaned. Gives a strike.
excusedExcused by the floor speaker: no strike, but it doesn't count as cleaned either.

Strikes

You get a strike for a missed or disputed duty, or from the floor speaker. If the floor speaker marks a missed duty as done afterwards or excuses it, the strike is dropped.

At {{ strike_threshold }} strikes you get a penalty trash duty: one week of trash duty that doesn't count as cleaned. After that the {{ strike_threshold }} strikes are gone. You can see your strikes under My profile.

Away

If you're away, e.g. on holiday, add it under My profile → Away. You won't be assigned during that time, and it doesn't count against you. A duty you already have stays yours, so add absences early; if something comes up at short notice, ask the floor speaker to excuse it. A penalty week moves to your next free week.

Dishwasher

When you've emptied the dishwasher, click I emptied it on the overview. After each entry nobody can mark it again for {{ dishwasher_cooldown }} minutes, so one load counts once.

The {{ dishwasher_heroes }} people who empty it most in a month become dishwasher heroes and get the whole next month off kitchen duty. It doesn't count against them, like being away. Trash duty stays as usual. On a tie, whoever reached that number first wins.

The statistics show this month's count. Only mark it when you really emptied it: the floor speaker can delete wrong entries from the current month.

Statistics

The "points" in the statistics are the duties you cleaned minus your fair share, 100 points per duty. Whoever has the fewest points is next. A positive number means you cleaned more than needed, a negative one that you still have duties to catch up on. All points add up to 0. A duty nobody did counts for nobody. Kitchen and trash are counted separately.
