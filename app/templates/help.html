How it works

On our floor we share two cleaning duties. This site splits them fairly and keeps track of who did what.

The duties

Kitchen duty: every day, tidy up and clean the shared kitchen. {% if kitchen_people > 1 %}{{ kitchen_people }} people are assigned, but only one of them has to clean. Talk to each other.{% endif %}

Trash duty: for one week (Monday to Sunday), take out the trash. {% if trash_people > 1 %}{{ trash_people }} people are assigned, one of them is enough.{% endif %}

The floor speaker will tell you what exactly is part of it.

When is it my turn?

The site assigns duties automatically: it's the turn of whoever has cleaned the least so far, relative to everyone else. If you're new, you start at zero.

The overview shows today's duties and the plan for the next {{ preview_days }} days. Your own name is highlighted. The plan can still change, e.g. when someone misses a duty or is away.

Each day's people are chosen at {{ deadline_hour }}:05, right after the deadline of the day before. Until then the overview still shows yesterday's kitchen duty.

As long as nobody from the current kitchen duty has marked it as done, the plan depends on who cleans. Above the kitchen preview, pick who cleans to see the plan for that case.

Marking a duty as done

Once you've cleaned, click Done next to your name on the overview. Only whoever actually cleaned marks it.

Deadline: {{ deadline_hour }}:00 {% if deadline_days_after == 1 %}on the day after the duty, so for trash duty the Monday after your week. {% else %}{{ deadline_days_after }} days after the duty.{% endif %} After that the duty counts as missed.

What do the statuses mean?

openNot marked yet, the deadline hasn't passed.
doneCleaned and marked.
not neededSomeone else on your duty cleaned. No strike, but it doesn't count as cleaned either.
missedNobody marked it by the deadline. Everyone assigned gets a strike.
disputedMarked, but not cleaned. Gives a strike.
excusedExcused by the floor speaker: no strike, but it doesn't count as cleaned either.

Strikes

You get a strike for a missed or disputed duty, or from the floor speaker. If the floor speaker marks a missed duty as done afterwards or excuses it, the strike is dropped.

At {{ strike_threshold }} strikes you get a penalty trash duty: one week of trash duty that doesn't count as cleaned. After that the {{ strike_threshold }} strikes are gone. You can see your strikes under My profile.

Away

If you're away, e.g. on holiday, add it under My profile → Away. You won't be assigned during that time, and it doesn't count against you. Duties that fall into that time are taken over by someone else right away.

Statistics

The "points" in the statistics are the duties you cleaned minus your fair share, 100 points per duty. Whoever has the fewest points is next. A positive number means you cleaned more than needed, a negative one that you still have duties to catch up on. All points add up to 0. A duty nobody did counts for nobody. Kitchen and trash are counted separately.
